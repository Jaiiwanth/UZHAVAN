# Design System: CropChain Lens
**Project ID:** 12833880850335622540

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
UZHAVAR OS CropChain Lens is an institutional-grade intelligence portal and crop passport platform built for farmers, agricultural cooperatives, and downstream market buyers. The brand personality balances uncompromising agronomic authority with immediate, human-centered accessibility. It projects grounded trust, technological transparency, and empirical precision without sterile corporate aloofness.

The design movement combines Modern High-Clarity Corporate with Tactile Agronomic Minimalism. It borrows structural cues from high-density fintech dashboards, pairing them with warm, produce-inspired real-world color signifiers and soft card elevations. Surfaces are crisp and legible under variable field lighting conditions.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Primary Foundation:** Obsidian midnight navy (`#0F172A` / `#131B2E`) for structural anchors, headers, and modal headers.
- **Accent & Interactive:** Harvest terracotta and vibrant orange (`#EA580C` / `#FD651E`) for decisive call-to-actions, active indicators, and sensitive triggers.
- **Functional States:** Leaf emerald (`#16A34A` / `#15803D`) for verified seals, certificates, positive margins, and pass states. Amber (`#B45309`) and flame (`#C2410C`) for audit flags and anomalies.

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Display figures, section titles, passport numbers, and primary badges.
- **Inter:** High-density tabular data, economic breakdown rows, field observation notes, and dialog content. Tabular figures (`tabular-nums`) applied to currency and quantities.
